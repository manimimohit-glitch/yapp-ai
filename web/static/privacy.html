Privacy & Data

Last updated: August 4, 2026 · Yapp.ai
The short version: we don't create accounts, we don't ask for personal info, and we don't keep your audio or your text. Both tools process data on the fly and then discard it.

✔ What we collect

Nothing personally identifiable. Yapp.ai has no accounts, no sign-up, and no tracking cookies. It doesn't run analytics scripts. Server logs keep only routine technical details (like request timestamps and sizes) that are needed to keep the service running.

▶ Text → Voice (your text)

When you type text and press Speak It, the text is sent to Microsoft's free neural-voice endpoint (via the edge-tts library) to generate the speech, and the MP3 streams back to your browser.

Your text is used to create your audio and is not stored or logged.

◉ Voice → Text (your audio)

When you record or upload audio, the file is sent to our server, where a Whisper model running on the server's CPU transcribes it. The file is written to a temporary location, processed, and then deleted immediately — even if an error occurs.

Yapp.ai does not store, save, or share your recordings. Any transcript downloads (.txt / .srt) are generated in your browser from the returned text.

≡ Hosting & uptime (the honest part)

Yapp.ai runs on Render's free tier. That keeps it free for you, but it comes with real limitations:

  • Cold starts — the instance sleeps after about 15 minutes of inactivity, so the first request after a pause can take 30–60 seconds to respond while it wakes up.
  • No uptime guarantee — free hosting can be slow or briefly unavailable, especially at busy times.
  • First transcription is slowest — the speech-to-text model loads on its first use, which adds extra delay on top of a cold start.

Please don't rely on this for anything time-critical.

≈ Rate limits

There are no hard limits today, and we only ask for fair use. Very long recordings take longer to transcribe because speech-to-text runs on a small CPU-only model. If you hit an error, waiting a moment and retrying usually fixes it.
